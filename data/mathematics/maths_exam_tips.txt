MATHEMATICS EXAM TIPS

Chapter: Sets

A Set is a well-defined collection of objects.

Types of Sets:

1. Empty Set
2. Finite Set
3. Infinite Set
4. Equal Sets
5. Equivalent Sets

Important Symbols:

∈  belongs to
∉  does not belong to
⊂  subset
⊆  subset or equal to

Common Mistakes:

- Confusing subset and proper subset.
- Forgetting braces while writing sets.

Exam Strategy:

Write definitions exactly as given in NCERT.
Practice Venn diagram questions regularly.
